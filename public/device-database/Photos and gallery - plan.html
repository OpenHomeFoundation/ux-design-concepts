Planning · Stage 02 increment

Photos and gallery: knowing the box when you see it

A device is a physical thing in a physical box, and half of recognising it is seeing it. This increment adds curated product and packaging photos: a gallery on the device page and lead thumbnails on Browse cards. The photos are reference imagery, held to the same evidence bar as every other enrichment. This is the plan for that, and for what a missing photo must never do.

Builds on Stage 02 · Richer device pages · pairs with Browse grid view

A photo is reference, not decoration. It shows the reader the exact box on the shelf and the device in hand, so the entry they are reading is the product they are holding. Photos are enrichment assets, curated and attributed like prose, and they are always optional: a device with none reads perfectly well, it just reads in words.

What it is

A gallery on the page, a lead on the card

Photos live as an array on the device record, contributed and curated like any Stage 02 field. They surface in two places. On the device page, a gallery: product shots and packaging, captioned, opened to full size. On Browse, the first photo becomes the card's lead thumbnail, which is what makes the grid view worth looking at.

On the device pagedetail.jsx · gallery

A gallery of curated shots: the product, the packaging, the ports. Reference imagery a reader checks against the thing in their hands, not a hero banner.

On Browsebrowse.jsx · lead thumbnail

The first photo leads the card, in the grid especially. Where there is no photo, a clean placeholder or text card stands in, never a broken frame.

With the increment off, the page and cards fall back to text, and nothing about the layout breaks. Photos are an addition over the record, not a dependency of it, which is exactly what lets the increment be toggled cleanly alongside its Stage 02 siblings.

The dependency

Independent of the grid, the reason the grid sings

Photos and Browse grid view are separate increments. Photos show up in the list and the gallery with the grid off, and the grid renders text cards with photos off. But the lead thumbnail is what a photo-forward grid is for, so the pair is where each one pays off most. Ship and judge them separately, expect them to compound.

Where we draw the line

Evidence imagery, never a storefront

Product and packaging, not marketing renders. The job of a photo is recognition: this is the box, this is the device. Lifestyle hero shots and glamour renders are the wrong register for a reference.

A missing photo is a graceful gap. No photo means a placeholder or a text card, never a hidden device and never a broken frame. Coverage will always be partial, and that is fine.

Photos are attributed like any edit. A contributed photo carries its contributor, the same provenance every Stage 02 field gets. Imagery is held to the evidence bar, not exempt from it.

The photo informs, it never ranks. Having a picture does not promote a device in Browse. The result order is the filter's, not the gallery's.

Recommendation

What to hold

  1. Treat photos as evidence. Curate product and packaging shots that help a reader confirm the device, and keep marketing imagery out. The register is a reference catalogue, not a shop.

  2. Design the empty state first. Most devices will start with no photo. The placeholder and text fallback are the common case, so make them clean, not an afterthought.

  3. Ship alongside the grid, measure together. The lead thumbnail is what the grid view was built around. The two increments are independent, but their value is clearest as a pair.
