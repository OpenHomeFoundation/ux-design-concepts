Planning · Stage 02

Richer device pages: a reading on top of the record

Foundation publishes the facts a report can stand behind. Stage 02 turns the bare entry into a page worth reading: a curated overview, references, a plain-language requirements matrix, and detailed specifications. The rule that makes it safe is that enrichment is a layer over the reported record, never a rewrite of it. This is the plan for that layer, and the three increments that hang off it.

Builds on Stage 01 Foundation · holds the line set by the editorial stance

Stage 02 adds a reading, it does not move the floor. Every enriched field is an overlay: turn the stage off and the page falls back to Foundation's reported content with nothing missing and nothing broken. Search still indexes only reported fields, the verdict still comes from the reported record, and a claim with no source still says so. The stage earns its place by making the same facts legible, not by inventing new ones.

What it adds · four enrichments

From a record to an article

A Foundation entry can tell you the verdict and the integration. It cannot tell you why, or what a setup actually demands. Stage 02 fills that in with four editorial fields, each authored, each citable, each optional. Where one is empty, the page shows the gap rather than papering over it.

EnrichmentWhat it carriesSource of truth
Curated overviewThe prose summary and description: how the device behaves and pairs, in a wiki editor's voice.summary · description
ReferencesManufacturer pages and documentation a reader can check. Citations, not marketing links.specs.references
Requirements matrixSetup, day-to-day control, and offline behaviour, in plain language.specs.connectivity
Detailed specificationsCategory specs, protocols, dimensions, identifiers, the figures.specs.*

The figures on the page are derived or contributor-entered, never decorative. The connection schematic is drawn straight from the reported local-or-cloud verdict, so it cannot disagree with the headline. The requirements matrix reads specs.connectivity and shows a plain "-" on any row a contributor has not filled in. The firmware timeline is just the version history Foundation already collects, drawn out.

The seam · what stays untouched

Enrichment overlays, it never overwrites

The whole stage rests on one boundary, the same one Foundation draws: reported facts versus authored reading. Stage 02 only ever adds on the authored side. Three things on the reported side are deliberately left alone, and that is what lets the stage be switched off cleanly.

Search is not enrichedsearchbox.jsx · reported corpus

The index stays names, manufacturers, models, and categories. A committed search can never surface a device on the curated summary, so toggling the stage never changes what Browse finds.

The verdict is not re-derivedthe reported record

Local, cloud, and Home Assistant support still come from Foundation. The overview explains the verdict, it never sets it. The schematic renders the reported answer, not an editor's.

The third is uncertainty. Stage 02 inherits Foundation's honesty rule rather than smoothing it over: an unverified claim is marked unverified, and a field with no contributor input reads "-" instead of a confident guess. A richer page is not a more certain one.

The path

A core that defines the stage, then three independent children

The core is the enriched read view: overview, references, requirements, and specs on the device page. Ship it and Stage 02 exists. The three increments each add a surface that builds on the core and on nothing else, which is why each can be switched off on its own without collapsing the stage. They are independent of one another by design.

Where we draw the line

A reference page, never a review

Richer pages are the place the editorial stance is most tempting to break. More room invites opinion. These rails keep the extra space factual.

Verbs over adjectives. "Exposes 14 entities," not "powerful entity support." The overview describes behaviour, never quality, and never ranks one device against another.

References are citations, not links to buy. Manufacturer docs and source a reader can check. No affiliate, no sponsored placement, no store.

A gap reads as a gap. An empty requirement shows "-", an unverified claim is marked. Enrichment never manufactures a fact to fill the layout.

The floor is reachable with the stage off. Every enriched field is an overlay over the reported record, so Foundation always shows through underneath.

Recommendation

What to hold, in order

  1. Keep the core and the children separable. The enriched page must stand without grid view, photos, or multi-ecosystem Connects with. If a child cannot be switched off cleanly, it has crept into the core.

  2. Never let enrichment reach into search. The reported corpus is the contract with Foundation. The day a curated summary changes a search result, the floor has moved.

  3. Derive the figures, never decorate. The schematic and timeline must read from reported data so they cannot contradict the headline. A figure that asserts its own fact is a bug.

  4. Treat the three increments as separate decisions. Each has its own plan and its own toggle. Ship and evaluate them one at a time, not as a bundle.
