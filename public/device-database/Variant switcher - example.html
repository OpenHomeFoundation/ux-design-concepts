Stage 04 · model mock, revised

Variants and families, after the research

The model inverted. We are no longer fitting devices into a clean two-axis grid. The research found about fourteen buying-fact axes, a third of product lines that do not factor at all, and firmware as the second most decisive axis. So enumeration is now the substrate, and the tidy views sit on top of it.

What changed: enumerated variant rows are the truth, axes are derived from them, the verdict carries a firmware range, region splits into several facts, identity keys let data arrive with no contribution, and the decisive axis is read per family rather than assumed to be protocol.

01

The picker is generated from the variants that exist

The switcher is a view over the enumerated rows, not a free cross-product. Axes and their values are read off the rows, single-value axes are hidden, and only combinations that shipped are offered, so a buyer cannot reach a variant that never existed. The verdict it resolves to is now scoped to a firmware range and a last-verified date, because firmware can flip the verdict with no purchasable change.

Three ways a variant gets its data

Not everything is a contribution. A variant can be observed from telemetry, mapped from an identity key with nobody writing a word, or authored by a contributor. The first two carry a truthful record on their own. In the family list below, the EU Matter row was mapped and is honest but thin, while the US rows have been authored.

03

Find your exact variant by its identity key

For roughly a third of devices the box cannot tell you which one you hold: a Zigbee model string is only visible after pairing, and white-label hardware is not identifiable from the outside at all. So besides the switcher, a reader can match on a machine key, the same keys the Mapped path uses. Recording them is a service the packaging does not provide.

04

Region is not one axis, and power waits for it

A single region enum lies. Australia is 230 V like the EU but 921 MHz, unlike it, so region decomposes into RF band, mains voltage, plug type, and certification, each its own bound fact. And the page still refuses to print any of it until a region is resolved, because the wrong electrics is the one fact this project will not publish.

05

The decisive axis is read per family, not assumed

Yale flips on the radio module. But Kasa flips on hardware revision, Eve on firmware, Hue on hub mode, Reolink on power source. So the UI cannot hard-code "protocol is the important axis", it marks whichever axis carries the verdict on each family. The matrix appears only when a family factors on two axes and the verdict actually varies.
