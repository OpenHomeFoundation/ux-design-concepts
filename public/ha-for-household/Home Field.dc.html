Home field
Two forces painted as one image, in the spirit of Zeh Fernandes' GenCup posters. Inside (heating, light, occupancy, activity) blooms from the center outward; outside (temperature delta, wind, weather, natural light) presses inward from the edges. The vertical axis is the home's last 24 hours, and the sun's arc tones the canvas. A large inside-to-outside delta makes the field dense and tense; near equilibrium it softens and thins; nobody home leaves a dead zone. Painted once, like a poster, from the home's own data.
01 / empty home, away all day
Nobody home for a day. Heating and lights off, so inside and outside drift into equilibrium. The field thins to almost nothing, a dead zone, leaving the sun's arc and the bare grid.
02 / one person, evening in
A quiet day, one person home after dark. Warmth blooms from the center through the evening, a small contested pocket against the cool pressing in. Calm the rest of the day.
03 / full house, crowded evening
A busy day building to a crowded evening. Heating and lights drive a wide, tense band low on the day; the large inside-to-outside delta makes the strokes dense and swirling.
04 / quiet Sunday morning
A gentle morning, mild outside. Inside and outside sit near balance, so the field stays soft and thin, a warm rise through the late morning over a cool, bright canvas.
